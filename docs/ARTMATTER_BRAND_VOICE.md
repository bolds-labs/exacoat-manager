# Artmatter Brand Voice & Writing System

> **The Canonical Standard for Product, Manager, and Customer Copy**
> 
> *Artmatter is a premium art platform. The language should feel quiet, refined, confident, human, and understated.*

---

## 1. Core Voice & Philosophy

Artmatter speaks with the voice of a curated art studio and gallery. It handles deep technical and operational complexity behind the scenes, but the interface and communication never advertise that complexity.

### Artmatter sounds:
* **Quiet & Considered**: Confident without shouting. Every word earns its place.
* **Refined & Artistic**: Thoughtful, elegant, and tasteful.
* **Human & Reassuring**: Warm, authentic, and professional.
* **Minimal & Clear**: Says less, communicates more.

### Artmatter NEVER sounds:
* **Corporate or Enterprise SaaS**: Avoid ERP, logistics, CRM, and corporate jargon.
* **Overly Enthusiastic or Salesy**: No exclamation marks, hype, or artificial buzzwords.
* **Robotic or Algorithmic**: No developer-centric error codes, schema talk, or database names in user-facing copy.
* **Over-explained**: If the customer doesn’t need to know internal shop-floor mechanics, do not describe them.

---

## 2. Fundamental Writing Principles

### Rule 1: Say less, but make every word feel intentional
Do not add words simply to sound sophisticated. If a 2-word phrase communicates clearly, never use a 6-word sentence.

### Rule 2: Zero Exclamation Marks
Artmatter does not use exclamation marks (`!`) in product, manager, transactional emails, toast messages, or marketing copy.
* ❌ *Your artwork is ready!*
* ❌ *Refund processed successfully!*
* ❌ *Order Dispatched & Shipped!*
* ✅ *Your artwork is ready.*
* ✅ *Refund processed.*
* ✅ *Order shipped.*

### Rule 3: Avoid Operational & Logistics Jargon
Replace logistics and software terminology with natural, studio-appropriate language:

| ❌ Avoid (Operational / ERP) | ✅ Prefer (Artmatter Voice) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Customer Order Confirmed (Processing) | **Order Confirmed** |
| Production Fulfillment Workflow | **In Production** |
| Order Prepared & Awaiting Courier Pickup | **Ready to Ship** |
| Order Shipped & Courier Tracking Dispatched | **Shipped** / **On its way** |
| Transaction Completed Successfully | **Payment confirmed** |
| Creative Asset Repository | **Artwork** / **Designs** |
| Artist Relationship Management | **Artists** |
| Financial Settlement Operations | **Payouts** |
| Content Submission Pipeline | **Submissions** |
| Asset Ingestion / Upload Completed | **Artwork added** |
| Courier Collection | **Courier pickup** |
| AWB Tracking Synchronization | **Tracking** |

### Rule 4: Always use "Artist" — NEVER use "Creator"
In Artmatter's brand vocabulary, participants on the platform are always **Artists**. Never use the term "Creator".

| ❌ Never Use | ✅ Always Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| Creator Studio | **Artist Studio** |
| Creator Application | **Artist Application** |
| Creator Onboarding | **Artist Onboarding** |
| Creator Profile | **Artist Profile** |
| Creator Performance | **Artist Performance** |
| Community Creator | **Community Artist** / **Artist** |
| Approved Creator | **Approved Artist** |

## 3. Order Status Hierarchy & Customer Lifecycle

Use this terminology consistently across WordPress, Manager ERP, and Customer Emails:

| Status Code | Manager UI Badge | Customer Email Subject | Customer Email Heading | Customer Email Body |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `processing` | **Order Confirmed** | *Your Artmatter order is confirmed* | **Order confirmed** | *Thank you for your order. We’ve received it and will begin preparing your artwork shortly.* |
| `in-production` | **In Production** | *Your Artmatter order is in production* | **In production** | *Your artwork is now being prepared in our studio.* |
| `awaiting-pickup`| **Ready to Ship** | *Your Artmatter order is ready to ship* | **Ready to ship** | *Your order is ready to leave our studio.* |
| `shipped` / `completed` | **Shipped** | *Your Artmatter order has shipped* | **On its way** | *Your order has left our studio and is now on its way to you.* |
| `delivered` | **Delivered** | *Your Artmatter order has arrived* | **Delivered** | *Your order has been delivered. We hope it finds a good place in your space.* |
| `refunded` | **Refunded** | *Refund confirmation for your order* | **Refund processed** | *We have processed a refund of [amount] for your order.* |

## 4. Manager UI Voice (`manager.artmatter.co`)

The manager is an **internal studio workspace**, not an enterprise dashboard.

### Navigation & Section Names
* ✅ **Artists** (Not *Artist Management / CRM*)
* ✅ **Orders** (Not *Order Processing Queue*)
* ✅ **Artwork** or **Designs** (Not *Creative Asset Vault / Repositories*)
* ✅ **Collections** (Not *Categorized Product Portfolios*)
* ✅ **Payouts** (Not *Financial Disbursement Operations*)
* ✅ **Audit Logs** (Not *Telemetry Stream Monitoring*)
* ✅ **Settings** (Not *System Configuration Panel*)

### UI Actions & Buttons
* ✅ **Save Tracking Details** (Not *Dispatch Logistics AWB*)
* ✅ **Add Artwork** (Not *Upload Creative Asset*)
* ✅ **Issue Refund** (Not *Execute WooCommerce Reversal Gateway*)
* ✅ **Add Note** (Not *Insert Audit History Annotation*)
* ✅ **Save Changes** (Not *Commit Configuration Payload*)

## 5. Creator Studio Voice (`artist.artmatter.co`)

The creator experience is named **Artmatter Creator Studio** (short: **Creator Studio**). It is a professional creative workspace, not an administrative backend.

### Navigation Structure
* **Studio**:
  * **Overview** (Not *Dashboard*)
  * **Artwork** (Not *My Portfolio* or *Asset Vault*)
  * **New Artwork** or **Add Artwork** (Not *Upload Studio*)
  * **Collections** (Not *Series Albums*)
* **Performance**:
  * **Earnings** (Not *Earnings & Sales Ledger*)
  * **Payouts** (Not *Payout Claims*)
* **Profile**:
  * **Creator Profile** (Not *Profile & KYC*)

## 6. Customer Email Standards

All customer emails sent by Artmatter must follow these standards:
1. **Tone**: Short, elegant, reassuring, and useful.
2. **Clarity**: 
   - What happened.
   - What happens next.
   - Whether they need to take action (e.g. tracking link).
3. **No Fluff**: No "Congratulations", no repeated order numbers in every paragraph, no generic plugin phrases.
4. **Clean CTA**: Direct and unpretentious (e.g. `Track your order`, `View order details`).

## 7. Artwork & Artist Terminology

Artmatter represents fine art and premium craftsmanship:
* Use: **Artwork, Piece, Design, Artist, Collection, Edition, Studio, Gallery, Curated, Selected.**
* Avoid: **Asset, Content, Upload, Item, Resource, Creative, SKU.**

### Artist Profile Language
* Artists may write application biographies in the language they know best.
* Recommend English for wider storefront reach, but never require it as the only accepted language.
* Use **Translate to English** for assisted translation actions.
* Use the `✦` symbol for artist-facing AI assistance. Do not use a sparkle icon.
* Preserve the artist's original meaning and voice, and let the artist review translated copy before saving.

## 8. Mandatory Directive for Agents & Developers

Before implementing or modifying any customer-facing, manager-facing, or creator-facing feature:
1. Read `docs/ARTMATTER_BRAND_VOICE.md`.
2. Apply its terminology and writing principles.
3. Eliminate all exclamation marks and ERP-style copy.
4. If introducing new vocabulary, update this file so it remains the canonical standard.
