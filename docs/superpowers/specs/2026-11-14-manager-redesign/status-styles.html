Making statuses easy to tell apart

The trick is not more colours, it is giving each colour one meaning: what the status asks you to do. Nine statuses fall into five groups, so you can scan a column and see at a glance which orders need you. Below, the same statuses in three styles. Pick the one you like.

Needs actionYou must do something: to prepare, pending review
WaitingWaiting on the customer: awaiting payment
In progressMoving along: preparing, ready for pickup, shipped
DoneFinished: completed, claim approved
StoppedCancelled, refunded, claim rejected
